# Wave 1 — hospital procedures

Observer: team member hospital-procedures. Observed 2026-09-01.

## Key findings

- Korea University Guro publicly describes **remade** tissue slides that need not be returned, but original cytology materials that must be returned within one month. It also lists different patient/relative/proxy document sets and next-day versus one-week fulfilment depending on material. Source: https://guro.kumc.or.kr/kr/reservation-issue/service/pathological.do
- Korea University Anam publicly lists H&E and unstained material at 19,200 KRW each, while its timing text conflicts internally (two days vs next-day); this is a concrete reason to verify by telephone. Source: https://anam.kumc.or.kr/kr/reservation-issue/service/pathological.do
- CHA Gangnam and Bundang list unstained slides at 10,000 KRW and 20,000 KRW respectively, and both distinguish remade tissue slides from original cytology loans with a 50,000 KRW return deposit. Sources: https://gangnam.m.chamc.co.kr/appointment/issue/pathology.cha ; https://bundang.m.chamc.co.kr/appointment/certificate/pathology.cha
- Central University hospitals state that paraffin blocks are unavailable and that slides are re-reviewed before release; their unstained-slide prices include 20,000–21,000 KRW. Sources: https://ch.cauhs.or.kr/home/conts/102008000000000.do ; https://gh.cauhs.or.kr/home/conts/102008000000000.do
- Catholic hospitals at Yeouido and Daejeon explicitly state that paraffin-block loan is unavailable; Uijeongbu lists 9,000 KRW for an unstained ordinary-stain slide. Sources: https://www.cmcsungmo.or.kr/page/department/A/52/1 ; https://www.cmcdj.or.kr/m/cs/certificate_04.jsp ; https://www.cmcujb.or.kr/hospitalguide.clinic_data.sp
- LabGenomics’ public guide describes paraffin-block/original loan with a receipt and mandatory return. Source: https://www.labgenomics.com/data/file/m04_04/1935545628_o902DsgK_1e89f06753c198c5559116b2315e919d398d2c85.pdf

## Interpretation

The public evidence supports a three-way distinction: (1) remade material that is not returned, (2) original material temporarily loaned with return/deposit controls, and (3) material that an institution will not loan. It does **not** support a universal right to take 3–4 blocks.
