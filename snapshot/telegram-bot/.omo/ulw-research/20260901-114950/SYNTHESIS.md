# ULW-Research Synthesis: 국내 암 병리조직 반출과 비용 구조

Workers: 10 · Waves: 3 · Excursions: 0 · Sources used in report: 23 numbered references (19 unique domains) · Verifications: 1 independent high-risk wording audit + 2 independent PDF visual QA passes · Elapsed: 24 min

## Executive summary

Korean public hospital pages show that pathology material release is an institution-specific custody workflow, not a single “get the blocks” transaction. A patient has a statutory pathway to records/copies/content confirmation, but the reviewed text does not expressly grant permanent possession of original blocks. Hospitals may make new tissue/unstained slides, lend tracked original material subject to return, or refuse paraffin-block lending.

The economic premise is directionally plausible only if a particular pathology department actually quotes hundreds of newly prepared slides. Public prices vary by service label and are not a Korean standard. The exact 23,000-KRW endpoint was not verified. A 10-µm tube-scroll approach must be the recipient's written, current requirement; molecular and vaccine protocols materially differ.

## Findings by theme

- **Process:** patient/proxy verification, pathology review, production, payment, and if original material is involved, receipt/return tracking. [Sources 1–7, 11–12]
- **Costs:** do not normalize recuts, staining, loans, deposits, and record copies. Conditional 300-slide multiplication is shown as a scenario only. [Sources 5–10, 18–20]
- **Technical adequacy:** product-specific requirements may call for blocks, 5-µm slides, 10-µm slides, H&E, tumor percentage, and normal blood. [Sources 13–17]
- **Risk:** no universal original-block entitlement, no universal physical-retention rule established by the reviewed Medical Act text, and conditional research/cross-border compliance. [Sources 1–3, 21–23]

## Contradictions resolved

- “Block loan is available” versus “block loan is unavailable”: both occur, at different institutions; report presents this as variation.
- “10 µm is preferred” versus “5 µm only”: both occur in different workflows; report requires recipient-specific instructions.
- “15–23k per slide”: arithmetic is possible but a universal market-rate claim is unsupported; exact 23k was not verified.

## Gaps

No Korean provider-specific current tumor-vaccine specimen manual was publicly located. The decisive next evidence is the actual receiving laboratory’s controlled accession sheet and the originating pathology department’s written material-availability/price response.
