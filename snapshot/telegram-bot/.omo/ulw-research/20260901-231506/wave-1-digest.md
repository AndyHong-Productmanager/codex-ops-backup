# Wave 1 digest

## Core convergence

- Aging is an economic and care-capacity redesign: WHO projects 60+ population from 1.0 billion (2020) to 2.1 billion (2050), while OECD projects a sharp worker-to-older-person ratio change. [WHO; OECD]
- Clinical AI is strongest in bounded workflows; workflow integration and human response determine value. FDA authorization is not general patient-outcome evidence.
- Sarcopenia/frailty AI is mainly measurement/risk stratification. Exercise, nutrition and multifactorial falls-prevention have the outcome evidence.
- Care technology has credible narrow uses (fall-response orchestration, transfers, documentation); it is not a substitute for relationship or staffing.
- The report’s humanistic frame will treat spirituality as a concrete operating discipline: dignity, humility, presence and repair.
