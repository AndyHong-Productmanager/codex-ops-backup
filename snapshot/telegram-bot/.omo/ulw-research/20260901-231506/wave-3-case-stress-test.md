# Wave 3 — AI fall/frailty case stress test

## Concrete test

An AI fall score does not prevent a fall. An accountable service does: bedside verification, individual plan, available staff/resource escalation, geriatric/medication/rehab response, patient/family communication, monitoring, and repair after a failure.

The Seoul pragmatic ML-CDS study showed a non-significant total-fall change but fewer injurious falls in a bundled multidisciplinary workflow. This supports cautious pathway-level interpretation, not AI-only causation. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10079353/

## Wording correction

- Do not say “AI predicts falls, so it prevents falls.”
- Say “AI may identify changing risk; prevention occurs only when a tailored plan is reliably carried out.”
