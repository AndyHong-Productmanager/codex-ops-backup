# Design specification

Template: clean analyst register
Lineage: inline

## Tokens

- ink: #17212B
- paper: #F8F7F3
- accent: #0B6E69
- rule: #D6D3CA
- muted: #5D6770

## Typography

- Headings and body: Noto Sans KR, Pretendard, Arial, sans-serif
- Korean text: `word-break: keep-all; overflow-wrap: anywhere;`

## Layout

- A4 portrait PDF, 18 mm margins
- Timeline tables may continue on a fresh page
- Fixed figure containers with `object-fit: contain`

## Structure

1. 집행 요약
2. 식별 및 관계 판정
3. 시계열
4. 주제별 근거
5. 상충·미확인 주장
6. 방법과 한계
7. 번호 출처

## Figures

- One timeline figure and one relationship/evidence figure, only when evidence supports them.
- Each figure needs a Korean title, legend/caption, and cited data.

## Citations

- Sentence-level bracketed references: [S<n>]
- Numbers state MEASURED, ASSUMED, or DERIVED inline.
