# Wave 2 — charism-bearing core, capital, and differentiated participation

## Returns journaled

### Focolare / Economy of Communion

- The Focolare Movement is a private universal association of the faithful with canonical juridical personality; its General Assembly, President, co-President and General Council govern the core. Source: https://www.focolare.org/en/about-us/ and https://www.focolare.org/en/frequently-asked-questions/.
- EoC companies are described by Focolare as autonomous and independent, operating in free markets. Participation and profit sharing are voluntary, so this is a strong core + enterprise-network analogue but not a hard corporate control model. Source: https://www.focolare.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdc_Report_web_ENG.pdf and https://www.edc-online.org/en/imprese-alias/linee-per-condurre-un-impresa.html.
- Lead: obtain authoritative current General Statutes and national-association bylaws; investigate Lionello Industrial Park ownership.

### Schoenstatt

- General Statute arts. 8–12 establishes League, Federation and Institute in increasing obligation; Federations/Institutes are the animating core while League is broad participation. Source: https://schoenstatt.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/106.11-1-Generalstatut-September-2017-english.pdf.
- Arts. 37–40 permit a center to be held by an institute, federation or local-law private corporation under a written agreement; arts. 86–88 preserve economic autonomy and reject common movement assets. This is a strong tiered-commitment model, not evidence of a controlled commercial group.
- Lead: query specific entrepreneur initiatives and local corporate bylaws.

### Opus Dei

- The Statutes prescribe differentiated commitment, staged incorporation and continuing formation, but confine Prelature jurisdiction to its particular end. Source: https://opusdei.org/en/article/statutes-of-opus-dei-eng/.
- Statute §122 says the Prelature does not take on the technical or financial aspects of civil initiatives and ordinarily is not their owner. This is a useful separation rule, not a for-profit ownership template.

### Communion and Liberation / Compagnia delle Opere

- CL’s 2025 Constitutions govern a formal ecclesial core; CdO’s own statute establishes a legally separate Italian nonprofit association serving businesses, nonprofits and professionals. Sources: https://www.clonline.org/storage_files/articles-attachments/1766145044-Constitutions_2025.pdf and https://www.cdo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/statuto_cdo_nazionale.pdf.
- The core/enterprise-network separation is explicit; it also exposes risk of public confusion, related-party opacity, and political-network capture. Lead: analyze separation safeguards and audited transactions.

### Mondragon

- Official principles subordinate capital to work, use one-person-one-vote, and articulate inter-cooperation, education and local social transformation. Source: https://www.mondragon-corporation.com/en/about-us/.
- Its structure provides general assemblies, governance/monitoring/social councils and mutual finance, but has no canonical charism-membership mechanism. It is an economic operating-system analogue, not a spiritual-core analogue. Source: https://www.mondragon-corporation.com/people/site/assets/files/103207/annual-report-2023-ext.pdf.

### Christian hybrid enterprises / mission locks

- Scott Bader Commonwealth is a UK guarantee-company/charity-owned industrial group with constitution-based common trusteeship and governance; it is a hard ownership/anti-sale analogue with Quaker ethical roots. Source: https://www.scottbader.com/wp-content/uploads/5843_constitution-printed-dec-2014.pdf.
- Foundation/purpose ownership gives durable capital protection: Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung statute preserves foundation ownership; Novo Foundation’s share classes separate votes from economic capital; Bosch separates foundation capital ownership and industrial-trust voting. Sources: https://www.carl-zeiss-stiftung.de/fileadmin/mediamanager/downloads/240129_Statut_EN.pdf ; https://novonordiskfonden.dk/en/who-we-are/ownership/ ; https://bosch-stiftung.de/en/node/16509.
- Delaware Code §3556 validates a noncharitable purpose trust and enforcer, a jurisdiction-specific building block for mission-held voting shares. Source: https://www.delcode.delaware.gov/title12/c035/sc04/index.html.

### Layered participation and safeguards

- Core covenant, employment, investor/partner and network affiliation require different instruments; a covenant cannot erase labor, anti-discrimination, fiduciary or reporting protections. Sources: https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/section-12-religious-discrimination ; https://www.irs.gov/irb/2004-22_IRB ; https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/443/595/.
- Required safeguards: separate entities/accounts, disinterested approval of related-party transfers, explicit member classes, mission-protected reserved matters, due process and exit rights, independent reporting/safeguarding, and no coercive economic or spiritual control.
