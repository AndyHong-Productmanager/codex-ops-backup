# Verification economics

| Claim | Risk | Error cost | Verification path | Decision | Outcome | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate charter wording and legal status | High | Misstating a binding provision as a values statement | Retrieve issuer/registry charter or filing; corroborate history separately | Verify | Pending | Historical and current documents may differ |
| Canon-law/civil-law boundary | High | False claim that church law overrides company law | Read canons 1290 and 1296 with juridic-person provisions; counter-search for civil override | Verify | Civil and canonical effects are parallel, not automatically substitutive | Local law remains jurisdiction-specific |
| Charism-core plus enterprise comparison | High | False analogue produces coercive or unworkable governance | Compare formal constitutions and civil instruments across Schoenstatt, Focolare, Opus Dei, CL/CdO, Mondragon and mission-lock cases | Verify | No exact analogue; combination supported; single-template claim refuted | Public documents may omit private contracts |
