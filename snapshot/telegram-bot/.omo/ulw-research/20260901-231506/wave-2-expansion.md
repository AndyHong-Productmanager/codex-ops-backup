# Wave 2 — evidence ladder and thesis audit

## Evidence ladder

The deployment unit is not a model but a sociotechnical pathway. A high-stakes aging-care tool must pass: need/intended use → analytic validity → transportability → human-workflow usability → clinical utility → equitable outcomes → lifecycle value. FDA/IMDRF, WHO, NIST, DECIDE-AI and pragmatic counterexamples support this no-skip logic.

## Thesis audit

- Preserve human-centred AI as the normative end; do not falsely position it as obsolete.
- Refine the title to **Human-Centered AI Needs Answerable Institutions**.
- Define answerability as identifying the decision and grounds, receiving meaningful challenge, repairing harm, and changing or stopping the system.
- Translate spirituality to operating disciplines; it must not replace consent, evidence, rights, independent challenge, or remedy.

## EXPAND

- LEAD: test the thesis against an AI frailty/fall workflow — WHY: exposes whether “answerability” makes a concrete difference — ANGLE: published hospital deployment and workflow failure modes.
