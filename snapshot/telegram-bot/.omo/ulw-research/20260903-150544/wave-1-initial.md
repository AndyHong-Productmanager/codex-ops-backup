# Wave 1 — initial returns

- Falls burden: CDC 2023 US surveillance; USPSTF 2024 exercise and individualized multifactorial recommendations; Korean KDCA injury-surveillance context.
- Hip fracture: Korean nationwide cohort shows postoperative pneumonia is associated with higher 30-day, 90-day, and one-year mortality; causality is not established.
- Vision AI: 2016 independent real-home validation shows synthetic-video performance did not transfer reliably. Dyphi is an eSPPB/frailty measurement platform, not a CCTV fall-detection vendor.
- Sensing/gait: free-living sensor signals are promising but validation/outcome evidence remains limited.
- Sarcopenia: consensus staging and association with falls/fracture; no proof yet that treating sarcopenia itself reduces those outcomes.
- GLP-1: DXA lean-mass reduction occurs with major weight loss but is not a sarcopenia diagnosis.
