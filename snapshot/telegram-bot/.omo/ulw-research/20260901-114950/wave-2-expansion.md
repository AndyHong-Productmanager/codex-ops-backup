# Wave 2 — expansion

## Chain of custody / legal boundary

- National Cancer Center explains that outside-hospital unstained slides and paraffin blocks may be submitted for additional pathology to support diagnosis, treatment direction, and prognosis. This is clinical information, not a nationwide ownership or loan statute. Source: https://www.ncc.re.kr/faqView.ncc?bbsnum=1175&code=014_002
- Medical Act access is to patient records/copies/content confirmation. The reviewed retention rule gives five years for test-content/findings records, not an express FFPE block or glass-slide period. Sources: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?ancYnChk=&chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1007235937 ; https://law.go.kr/lumLsLinkPop.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=61681
- Research provision is distinct: Bioethics and Safety Act Articles 36–38 require an IRB/consent/provision framework where applicable. Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=199534

## Price counter-search

- No directly verifiable official Korean pathology price of exactly 23,000 KRW per slide was found. This is absence in the searched primary sources, not proof that no such price exists.
- Official postings demonstrate wide category-specific variation: 15,000 KRW for new unstained slide production at Konkuk; 20,000 KRW for an unstained slide at Bundang CHA; 22,000 KRW for external-block H&E at Incheon St. Mary’s; refundable deposits are a separate category. Sources: https://www.kuh.ac.kr/guide/request6.do ; https://bundang.m.chamc.co.kr/appointment/certificate/pathology.cha ; https://www.cmcism.or.kr/treat_guide/nonpay/?offset=420
